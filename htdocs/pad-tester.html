Hand-drawn Pattern Stability Test

Purpose: verify that "drawing roughly the same every time" reliably produces the same direction sequence.
Method: draw your pattern on the pad below (horizontal / vertical / diagonal segments are all fine, at least 10 segments, each reasonably long), click "Record this draw" (the pad clears automatically), repeat 10+ times and check the consistency rate.

💡 Warm up with 1-2 draws first until the feedback line shows "Valid" with the number of segments you expect; then record each draw from memory exactly the same way.
